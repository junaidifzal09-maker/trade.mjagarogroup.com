Pakistani Chicken Biryani

Learn how to make aromatic Pakistani chicken biryani with layered basmati rice, tender chicken and TAVAAZO Biryani Masala for consistent family-style flavour.

Prep25 minutes
Cook55 minutes
Serves6

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 750g bone-in chicken pieces
  • 500g basmati rice, rinsed and soaked for 30 minutes
  • 1 packet TAVAAZO Biryani Masala ↗
  • 250g plain yoghurt
  • 3 medium onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons TAVAAZO Ginger Garlic Paste ↗
  • 120ml cooking oil
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • A handful each of fresh mint and coriander
  • Optional: saffron or yellow food colour mixed with 3 tablespoons warm milk

TAVAAZO products used

Cook withBiryani MasalaCook withGinger Garlic Paste

STEP BY STEP

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil in a heavy pot and fry the sliced onions until deep golden. Reserve one-third for layering.

  2. 2

    Add the chicken and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in the ginger garlic paste, tomatoes, yoghurt and TAVAAZO Biryani Masala.

  3. 3

    Cover and cook over medium heat for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is tender and the masala is thick.

  4. 4

    Meanwhile, boil the soaked rice in salted water until about 70% cooked. Drain thoroughly.

  5. 5

    Spread half the rice over the chicken. Add mint, coriander, lemon and some reserved fried onion, then cover with the remaining rice.

  6. 6

    Drizzle over the saffron milk, top with the remaining fried onion and cover tightly.

  7. 7

    Steam on very low heat for 15–20 minutes. Rest for 5 minutes, then gently fold the layers and serve.
