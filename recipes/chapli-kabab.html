Pakistani Chapli Kabab

Make crisp-edged, juicy Pakistani chapli kabab with minced beef, fresh herbs and TAVAAZO Chapli Kabab Masala using this straightforward family recipe.

Prep25 minutes
Cook20 minutes
Serves5

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 750g minced beef with approximately 20% fat
  • 1 packet TAVAAZO Chapli Kabab Masala ↗
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped and squeezed dry
  • 2 medium tomatoes, deseeded and finely chopped
  • 1 egg
  • 4 tablespoons maize flour or gram flour
  • 2 green chillies, finely chopped
  • A handful of fresh coriander, chopped
  • Cooking oil for shallow frying
  • Lemon wedges and mint chutney to serve

TAVAAZO products used

Cook withChapli Kabab Masala

STEP BY STEP

Method

  1. 1

    Combine the minced beef, TAVAAZO Chapli Kabab Masala, onion, one chopped tomato, egg, flour, green chillies and coriander.

  2. 2

    Mix firmly for 3–4 minutes until the mixture becomes cohesive. Cover and rest in the refrigerator for 20 minutes.

  3. 3

    Divide into 10 portions. With damp hands, flatten each portion into a broad, thin patty and press a tomato slice or a little chopped tomato into the top.

  4. 4

    Heat a shallow layer of oil in a heavy frying pan over medium heat.

  5. 5

    Cook the kababs in batches for 4–5 minutes per side until browned, crisp at the edges and cooked through.

  6. 6

    Drain briefly and serve hot with naan, onion, lemon and mint chutney.
